FAQ

Questions we get asked most

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

We deliver around Kigali, Monday to Saturday, in a three-hour window. The fee depends on your area and is added to your total before you confirm. Delivery is free on orders over RWF 20,000.

When will my order arrive?

Order before 16:00 and it goes out on today's round. Later orders join the next dawn bake.

Can I collect my order instead?

Yes. Choose "Collect at the counter" at checkout. It is ready in about an hour at KG 11 Ave, Kicukiro, with no delivery fee.

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery, MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. Trade accounts can also pay by bank transfer.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can order without an account. Signing in only saves your address for next time.

Do your prices include VAT?

Yes. Retail prices are in Rwandan francs and include 18% VAT. Wholesale prices are quoted separately.

Which products contain nuts or allergens?

Nothing on the menu contains nuts except the groundnut cookies, which are made with groundnut paste. If you have an allergy, message us before you order.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Our sorghum and millet breads are a good place to start. Ask us about the gluten-free line if you need it kept separate.

Do you supply cafés, hotels and offices?

Yes. We offer standing orders with a trade price list. Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form for the wholesale price book.

What if my order is wrong or something is missing?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you notice, with your name and order details, and we will sort it out.
